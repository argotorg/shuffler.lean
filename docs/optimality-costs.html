Cost rules: SWAP and DUP use 3 gas and 1 byte. PUSH0 uses 2 gas and 1 byte. PUSHn uses 3 gas and n + 1 bytes. LOAD uses PUSHn for its address, then MLOAD: 6 gas and n + 2 bytes for n ≥ 1. Spill allocation is fixed. Memory expansion and spill stores are outside this comparison.

The trace witnesses and the listed costs at PUSH32 and at LOAD address widths 1 and 32 are checked in Tests/OptimalityCosts.lean. Changing the literal width selects another literal with that width. A weighted sum can omit Pareto points that lie above the lower convex hull. A complete Pareto search must retain all nondominated cost pairs.
